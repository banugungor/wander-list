Wanderlist Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Wanderlist ("the app") is developed as an independent, single-developer app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Data we collect

Wanderlist does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Everything you mark inside the app — visited World Heritage sites, visited countries, visited cuisines/dishes, and your recent activity history — is stored only on your own device, using standard local device storage. We have no access to this data, no analytics, and no account system that would let it leave your device.

2. Third-party services

To show reference photos and cuisine information, the app makes anonymous, unauthenticated requests to the following public APIs:

  • Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org) — to fetch public World Heritage Site images and summaries.
  • TheMealDB (themealdb.com) — to fetch public cuisine/meal reference data.

These requests do not include any personal information beyond what is inherent to a standard network request (e.g. your device's IP address, which is handled under each provider's own privacy policy).

3. Permissions

The app may request:

  • Internet access — to fetch the public content described above.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback when marking items visited.

4. Accounts

Wanderlist does not currently offer account creation, sign-in, or any cloud backup. No email, password, or authentication data is collected.

5. Children's privacy

Wanderlist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

6. Deleting your data

You can erase all locally stored app data at any time from Profile → "Verileri sıfırla" inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

8. Contact
